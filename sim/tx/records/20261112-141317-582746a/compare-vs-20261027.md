Findings:
- R4 steady-state swing is unchanged to within 8e-6 relative in all 54 corners; peak swing moves by at most 0.7 %. A pad capacitance changes edge rate, not DC swing, as expected, so R4 is insensitive to the pad C. The R4 load is now the extracted pad network in the deck, and R4 passes or fails exactly as before.
- R7 rise time (not in this issue's scope, shown for information) shifts by up to +10 %, because the ESD diodes add capacitance to the 70.66 fF metal.
- The failing measurements are the same four as before (`vc_max_pre_main_out{p,n}`, `vc_max_pre_post_out{p,n}`, a predriver collector limit): unchanged by the pad. No spec limit was touched.

Caveat carried from #141 (DR-013): PDK `dantenna`/`dpantenna` equivalence to the `diodevdd_2kv` clamp is not established (LVS #27); only the TX `padp` leg is covered. Not resolved here.
