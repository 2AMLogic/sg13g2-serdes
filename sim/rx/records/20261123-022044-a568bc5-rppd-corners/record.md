## Bench

The two ideal 50 ohm legs of `design/rx/netlists/rx_term_pad.spice` are replaced by PDK
`rppd` instances (r3_cmc OSDI via `osdi_preload`, as on the TX MOS benches): w=2u,
l=0.5015u, m=2 parallel strips. The model's resistance is R_side = rz + rsh*l/(m*(w+xw)),
with the terminal resistance rz = 35 ohm·um / w = 17.5 ohm (RCRPPD p.11, model
`rzspec`), in series with the channel and **not** divided by m -- sizing for the
channel term 50 - 17.5 = 32.5 ohm gives 50.000 ohm per side,
100.000 ohm differential at rsh = 260. The terminal term also compresses
the corner span: a +-10 % rsh move is about 65.0 % of the finished
resistor. w = 2 um is the p.11 precision recommendation; the layout minimums (0.50 um
width and SalBlock length) are met. Everything else is the pad_ac bench as-is:
PLACEHOLDER 50 fF pad capacitance, ideal 4 ohm metal series stand-ins, ideal
CTLE-internal resistors, code-0 CTLE hanging on the pad. |Zdiff| = 1/|I_diff| for a
1 V differential AC drive; the 0.1 GHz point stands in for the DC resistance.
